Connect on LinkedIn

One-time permission for linkedin.com

To prefill the invitation, the extension needs access to linkedin.com pages that it opens itself. You only grant this once.

What happens after you continue:

  1. The person's LinkedIn profile opens in a new tab.
  2. The extension clicks "Connect" and "Add a note" and fills in the note below.
  3. You read it and press "Send" yourself. The extension never sends anything on its own and never runs on LinkedIn pages you open yourself.

LinkedIn limits notes to 200 characters, and free accounts get only a few invitations with a note per month. You can edit the template in the extension's settings.
